# Getting Started

futureBank is the financial super-app for African university students.

## Download the App

futureBank is available on Android and iOS.

1. Visit **getfuturebank.vercel.app** on your phone
2. Tap **Download Free** — it detects your device automatically
3. Install and open the app

## Register

1. Open futureBank and tap **Create Account**
2. Enter your **university email** (e.g. `john@unima.ac.mw`)
3. Enter your **student ID number**
4. Create a **PIN** (6 digits)
5. Accept terms and conditions

## Verify Your Identity (KYC)

To use all features, you need to verify your identity:

1. Go to **Profile → Verify Identity**
2. Take a photo of your **student ID card** (front and back)
3. Take a **selfie** for biometric matching
4. Wait for verification (usually 2-5 minutes)

## Make Your First Deposit

1. Go to **Accounts → Deposit**
2. Select your preferred funding method (mobile money, bank transfer)
3. Enter the amount
4. Confirm

Your balance updates instantly. You're now ready to use futureBank.
