# Getting Started

## Download & Install

futureBank is available on Android and iOS. Download from the Google Play Store or Apple App Store.

## Registration

1. Open the app and tap **Create Account**
2. Enter your full name, university email address (or student ID number), and phone number
3. Create a secure PIN (6 digits)
4. Verify your email — we'll send a code to your university email
5. Choose your university from the list of supported institutions

## Identity Verification (KYC)

To unlock full account features, you'll need to verify your identity:
1. Go to **Profile → Verify Identity**
2. Take a clear photo of your student ID or national ID (front and back)
3. Take a live selfie for biometric matching
4. Your documents are reviewed — typically within minutes

Once verified, your daily limits increase and you unlock loan eligibility.

## Make Your First Deposit

1. Tap **Add Money** on the home screen
2. Choose your funding source:
   - **Mobile Money** (Airtel Money, TNM Mpamba) — instant
   - **Bank Transfer** — may take 1-2 business days
   - **Cash Deposit** at partner agents — instant
3. Enter the amount and confirm

Your balance updates immediately for mobile money and cash deposits.

## Start Using futureBank

Once you have funds, you can:
- **Send money** to any futureBank user instantly
- **Set a savings goal** and track your progress
- **Talk to the AI coach** for personalised financial advice
- **Join a savings challenge** with friends
- **Check your financial health score**
- **Apply for a micro-loan** (requires verified identity)
