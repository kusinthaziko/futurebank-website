# Security

Your money and data are protected by bank-grade security.

## Biometric Authentication

Use your **fingerprint** or **face** to log in. No passwords to remember. Biometric data never leaves your device.

## Screenshot Prevention

Sensitive screens (balance, KYC, transactions) are automatically protected from screenshots and screen recording.

## Auto-Lock

The app locks automatically after **5 minutes** of inactivity. Configure this in Settings (1, 5, or 15 minutes).

## Data Encryption

- All data on your device is encrypted with **AES-256**
- All network traffic uses **TLS 1.3** with certificate pinning
- Even on untrusted campus WiFi, your data is safe

## KYC Verification

Your identity is verified against institution records. Documents are stored securely and never shared.

## Fraud Protection

- Suspicious activity triggers **instant alerts**
- Large transactions require **additional verification**
- You can **freeze your account** instantly from Settings

## Tips to Stay Safe

- Never share your **PIN** with anyone
- futureBank will **never ask** for your password or PIN via message
- Enable **biometric login** for extra security
- Review your **transaction history** regularly
