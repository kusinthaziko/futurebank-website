# Accounts

futureBank gives you a full-featured campus bank account.

## Account Types

- **Savings Account** — Your main account. Earn interest on your balance.
- **Goal-Based Savings** — Set savings goals (e.g. "New laptop") and track progress.

## Viewing Your Balance

- Open the app to see your **dashboard** with current balance
- Tap the **eye icon** to hide/show your balance
- Pull down to **refresh**

## Account Number

Your account number is shown on the dashboard and in **Profile → Account Details**.

You can share this number to receive money from other students.

## Statements

1. Go to **Accounts → Statements**
2. Select a date range
3. Tap **Generate PDF**
4. Download or share the statement

## Transaction History

Every transaction is recorded in your history:

- Go to **Transactions** from the bottom nav
- Filter by **date**, **type**, or **amount**
- Tap any transaction for **details**
