# Frequently Asked Questions

## General

**What is futureBank?**
futureBank is a financial super-app for African university students. Savings, transfers, micro-loans, and AI-powered financial coaching — all in one app.

**Is futureBank free?**
Yes. The app is free to download and use. Some services (like loans) have associated fees which are clearly shown before you confirm.

**Which universities are supported?**
UNIMA, MZUNI, Lilongwe University, Polytechnic, LUANAR, DMI St John, Malawi College of Health Sciences, MUST, Catholic University, and more being added.

## Account

**How do I close my account?**
Go to **Profile → Settings → Close Account**. Withdraw any remaining balance first. Closed accounts can be reopened within 30 days.

**Can I have multiple accounts?**
Yes. You can open additional goal-based savings accounts.

## Transfers

**How fast are transfers?**
Instant — the money arrives in seconds.

**What if I send to the wrong person?**
Transfers are instant and irreversible. Always double-check the account number before confirming.

## Loans

**What happens if I can't repay?**
Contact support immediately. We can discuss a revised repayment plan. Late payments affect your financial health score.

**Can I pay off my loan early?**
Yes, with no penalty.

## Security

**What if my phone is stolen?**
Log in to futureBank from another device and freeze your account immediately. Contact support to recover your account.

**Is my data shared with third parties?**
Never. Your data is encrypted and private. We do not sell or share personal information.
