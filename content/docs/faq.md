# Frequently Asked Questions

## General

**What is futureBank?**
futureBank is a financial super-app built for African university students. It combines savings, instant peer-to-peer transfers, micro-loans, AI-powered financial coaching, and gamified challenges — all in one mobile app. No traditional bank account required.

**Who founded futureBank?**
futureBank was founded by **Redson Ngwira** and **Timothy Chalira** — two Malawian developers who experienced firsthand the struggle of managing finances as university students. They built futureBank to solve the financial exclusion that African students face.

**Why was futureBank created?**
Traditional banking doesn't work for students. High minimum balances, physical branch requirements, lack of credit history — these barriers lock students out of the financial system. futureBank was built to change that. It's a mobile-first, student-first financial platform that meets students where they are.

**Why should I use futureBank instead of a traditional bank?**
- Zero monthly fees and no minimum balance requirements
- Open an account in under 5 minutes with just your university email or student ID
- Get access to micro-loans even without a credit history — futureBank uses alternative scoring based on your activity and financial health
- AI coach that helps you budget, save, and build financial literacy
- Earn badges and compete in challenges as you hit savings goals
- Send and receive money instantly to any other futureBank user

**Is futureBank free?**
Yes. The app is free to download and use. Savings, transfers, and the AI coach are all free. Some services (like loans) have associated fees which are clearly shown before you confirm. No hidden charges.

**How is futureBank different from other mobile money services?**
Unlike mobile money (Airtel Money, TNM Mpamba), futureBank is designed specifically for students. It offers:
- AI-powered financial coaching personalised to your spending
- Savings goals with progress tracking
- Financial health scoring
- Group savings challenges with friends
- Micro-loans tailored to student needs
- Integration with your university life

**How does futureBank make money if it's free for students?**
futureBank earns through small fees on optional services like loans and premium features. We believe that financial tools should be accessible, not a source of stress. Our focus is on building long-term relationships with students as they grow into professionals.

**The future of fintech in Africa — where does futureBank fit?**
Africa has the youngest population in the world, with over 60% under 25. Yet less than 20% of Malawians have a traditional bank account. The future of finance is mobile-first, AI-driven, and designed for the way young people actually live. futureBank is part of this shift — building financial infrastructure for the next generation of African professionals.

By 2030, over 30 million African students will need financial services. futureBank is building for that future.

## Account

**How do I open an account?**
Download the app, register with your university email or student ID, verify your identity, and you're ready. No branch visits, no paperwork. Full steps in the getting-started guide.

**How do I close my account?**
Go to **Profile → Settings → Close Account**. Withdraw any remaining balance first. Closed accounts can be reopened within 30 days.

**Can I have multiple accounts?**
Yes. You can open additional goal-based savings accounts for different goals — travel, gadgets, school fees — each with its own progress tracker.

**Is my money safe?**
Yes. Accounts are protected with biometric authentication, PIN codes, and end-to-end encryption. We use industry-standard security practices and never share your data with third parties.

**What if I lose my phone?**
Log in to futureBank from another device and freeze your account immediately under **Profile → Settings → Freeze Account**. Contact support to recover or transfer your account to a new device.

## Transfers

**How fast are transfers?**
Instant — the money arrives in seconds. Whether you're splitting lunch with a friend or receiving money from family, it's immediate.

**Can I send money to someone who doesn't have futureBank?**
Currently transfers work between futureBank users. They can register in under 5 minutes using their university email.

**What if I send to the wrong person?**
Transfers are instant and irreversible. Always double-check the recipient details before confirming. FutureBank shows the recipient's name and university for verification.

**Are there transfer limits?**
Yes, limits scale with your verification level. Basic accounts have lower daily limits. Fully verified accounts have higher limits. See the transfers guide for details.

## Loans

**Can I get a loan as a student?**
futureBank offers micro-loans tailored for students. No collateral, no credit history required. Eligibility is based on your financial health score, account activity, and verification level.

**How do I apply for a loan?**
The AI coach can check your eligibility instantly. If eligible, funds are disbursed to your account immediately. You don't need to visit a bank or fill out lengthy paperwork.

**What happens if I can't repay?**
Contact support immediately. We can discuss a revised repayment plan. Late payments affect your financial health score and may affect future loan eligibility. We work with you, not against you.

**Can I pay off my loan early?**
Yes, with no penalty. Early repayment improves your financial health score.

**What are the loan limits?**
Loan limits start small and grow as you build your financial health score and repayment history. New users typically qualify for smaller amounts. Consistent repayment unlocks higher tiers.

## AI Coach

**What can the AI coach do?**
The AI coach is your personal financial assistant. It can:
- Check your account balance and recent transactions
- Analyse your spending patterns and suggest budgets
- Help you set and track savings goals
- Check your financial health score
- Assess loan eligibility
- Give personalised financial advice based on your actual data

**Is the AI coach private?**
Yes. The AI coach only accesses data you authorise. Your financial data is encrypted and never shared. The AI is powered by futureBank's secure systems, not third-party services.

## Security

- PIN-protected transactions
- End-to-end encryption

- Real-time fraud detection
- No data sharing with third parties

Never. Your data is encrypted and private. We do not sell or share personal information with anyone.
