# AI Financial Coach

Your personal financial advisor, 24/7, for free.

## What It Can Do

- **Track spending** — "How much did I spend on food this month?"
- **Savings advice** — "How can I save more for a laptop?"
- **Budget help** — "Create a weekly budget for MWK 30,000"
- **Goal tracking** — "Am I on track for my savings goal?"
- **Financial insights** — "Where can I cut costs?"

## How to Use

1. Tap the **AI Coach** icon on the dashboard
2. Type or speak your question
3. Get a personalised answer based on your actual transactions

Or use the **quick questions** to get started.

## Privacy

- The AI only sees your transaction **categories and amounts**, not merchant names
- Your data is **never used to train external AI models**
- You can **delete your chat history** anytime in Settings

## Tips for Best Results

- Be specific: "How much on food this week?" gets better answers than "Am I spending too much?"
- The more you use the app, the smarter the coach becomes
- Check in weekly for the best financial insights
