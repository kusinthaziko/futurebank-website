# About futureBank

## Our Story

futureBank was founded by **Redson Ngwira** and **Timothy Chalira**, two Malawian developers who experienced the pain of managing finances as university students firsthand.

As students, they faced the same barriers millions of African students deal with every day:
- Banks required minimum balances they couldn't afford
- Opening an account meant visiting a branch during lecture hours
- No credit history meant no access to loans, even small ones
- Mobile money was useful but not designed for students

They built futureBank to solve this — a financial platform designed from the ground up for African university students.

## Our Vision

**A future where every African student has access to fair, intelligent financial tools.**

Africa has the youngest population in the world — over 60% under 25 years old. Yet traditional financial systems exclude young people by design. We believe that being a student shouldn't mean being locked out of the financial system.

By 2030, over 30 million African students will need financial services. We're building the infrastructure for that future.

## Why Students Need futureBank

### The Problem
- **80%+ of Malawians don't have a traditional bank account**
- Students have no credit history, so banks won't lend to them
- Minimum balance fees eat into limited student budgets
- Financial literacy resources are scarce and often not practical
- Existing mobile money services are general-purpose, not student-focused

### The Solution

futureBank addresses these problems directly:

**Zero barriers to entry.** Open an account with just your university email. No minimum balance, no monthly fees, no branch visits.

**AI-powered financial coaching.** The AI coach analyses your actual spending, suggests budgets, tracks savings goals, and gives personalised advice — like having a financial adviser in your pocket.

**Micro-loans for students.** We use alternative credit scoring based on your financial health and account activity, not traditional credit history. Students can access small loans for books, transport, or emergencies.

**Gamified savings.** Set goals, track progress, earn badges, and compete in challenges with friends. Saving becomes engaging, not a chore.

**Instant peer-to-peer transfers.** Send money to any futureBank user instantly. Split bills, share costs, receive money from family — all in seconds.

**Financial health scoring.** Your financial health score gives you a clear picture of your financial wellbeing and improves as you build healthy habits.

## The Future of Fintech in Africa

The African fintech revolution is just beginning. Mobile money penetration has grown from near-zero to over 20 million registered wallets in Malawi alone. The next wave is about **intelligent financial services** — AI-driven, personalised, and built for the way young people live.

Key trends that futureBank is part of:
1. **AI-driven financial wellness** — apps that coach, not just transact
2. **Alternative credit scoring** — using behaviour data, not collateral
3. **Gamification** — making financial literacy engaging and rewarding
4. **Mobile-first, mobile-only** — designed for smartphones, not branches
5. **Community features** — group savings, challenges, peer support

futureBank is at the intersection of all these trends, built specifically for the continent's most important demographic: its students.
