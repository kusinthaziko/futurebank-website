# Transfers

Send money to any student on campus in seconds.

## Send Money

1. Tap **Send** on the dashboard or go to **Transfers → Send**
2. Enter the **recipient's account number** or select from contacts
3. Enter the **amount**
4. Add an optional **note** (e.g. "For lunch")
5. Confirm with your **PIN**

The money arrives instantly. Both you and the recipient get a notification.

## Receive Money

Share your account number with anyone. They can send you money through the app.

You'll receive a notification the moment money lands in your account.

## Transaction Limits

| Tier | Single Transfer | Daily Limit |
|------|----------------|-------------|
| Unverified | MWK 20,000 | MWK 50,000 |
| KYC Verified | MWK 100,000 | MWK 300,000 |

## Transaction Receipt

Every transaction generates a **digital receipt** (PDF):

1. Go to **Transactions**
2. Tap the transaction
3. Tap **Download Receipt**
4. Share or save the PDF

## Cancelling a Transfer

Transfers are **instant and irreversible**. Always double-check the recipient's account number before confirming.
