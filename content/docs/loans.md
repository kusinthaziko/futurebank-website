# Loans

Apply for campus micro-loans in minutes.

## Eligibility

- Must be a **registered student** at a partner university
- Must have completed **KYC verification**
- Must have an **active account** for at least 30 days
- **Good financial health score** (500+)

## How to Apply

1. Go to **Loans** from the dashboard
2. Tap **Apply for a Loan**
3. Enter the **amount** you need
4. Choose your **repayment period**
5. Review the **interest rate** and **fees**
6. Confirm with your **PIN**

You'll get an instant decision in most cases.

## Repayment

- Repayments are **automatic** from your account balance on the due date
- You can **repay early** with no penalty
- Go to **Loans → My Loans** to see your repayment schedule

## Late Payment

If you miss a repayment:
- Your **financial health score** drops
- Late fees apply (5% of the overdue amount)
- You won't be able to apply for new loans until cleared

## Loan Limits

| Tier | Max Loan Amount |
|------|----------------|
| Bronze (score 500-699) | MWK 50,000 |
| Silver (score 700-899) | MWK 150,000 |
| Gold (score 900+) | MWK 300,000 |
